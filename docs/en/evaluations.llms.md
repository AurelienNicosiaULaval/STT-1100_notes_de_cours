# Assessments

This page gives preparation landmarks. Official weights and deadlines published on MonPortail remain the reference.

## Types of traces

### Adventures

Guided activities to learn technical moves and reasoning.

### Challenges or exercises

More autonomous work to check mastery of the tools.

### Session project

A more integrated production combining data, analysis, visualization and communication.

## Cross-cutting criteria

- Reproducibility of the work.
- Clarity of code and explanations.
- Relevance of visualizations.
- Careful interpretation of results.
- Respect for collaboration and ethics instructions.
