## How to work through a module

Modules follow a stable structure. That repetition is intentional: it helps you know where to find instructions, where to practise and where to produce a trace.

1

### Plan and readings

Start with the learning plan, complete the targeted readings, then check key ideas with the mini-test.

2

### Adventure

Work in class through a guided situation: role, data, code, interpretation and decisions to explain.

3

### Challenge

Extend the adventure in the same universe with more autonomy and an assessed deliverable.

4

### Exercises

Consolidate technical skills in other contexts. Exercises are separate from the challenge narrative.

## Pathway progression

The course advances in layers. Skills come back several times, but the data, responsibilities and products become more autonomous.

Modules 1-3

### Enter the practice

RStudio, Quarto, first reports, GitHub, visualization and categories.

Modules 4-6

### Make work reliable

Cleaning, relationships between variables, joins, documentation and collaboration.

Modules 7-8

### Act responsibly

Responsible visualization, ethics, anonymization and careful automation.

Modules 9-10

### Share with judgment

Prediction, bias, text, dashboarding and communication of results.

## Quick landmarks

Use these landmarks when you are not sure which page to open.

### I am starting the week

Open the current module, read the learning plan and identify required readings, data and files.

### I am working in class

Open the module adventure. It is the main guided path for the session.

### I am preparing a submission

Open the challenge, check the expected format and confirm administrative requirements on Brio.

### I want more practice

Open the exercises. They consolidate skills in other contexts and with other datasets.

### I am technically stuck

Use the toolkit, class supports and [AI page](ia.llms.md) to reduce the problem. In [GPT STT-1100](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-682d165f32e881918633affa3fe9dfd6-gpt-stt-1100), ask: *Here is the full error message, my minimal code and what I expected. What is the most likely cause and what simple test should I run?*

### I need the general order

Use the calendar to see the session path and milestones, then return to the current module.
