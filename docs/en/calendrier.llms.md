## How to read the calendar

Each module follows the same general cycle. Details can vary by session, but the work order remains stable.

1

### Before

Open the learning plan, complete the targeted readings and use the mini-test to check key ideas.

2

### During

Work through the adventure in class: role, data, code, interpretation and decisions to justify.

3

### After

Complete exercises to consolidate in other contexts and prepare the challenge when the module requires it.

4

### Trace

Render documents, check files, push the repository if needed and keep reusable learning.

## Week-by-week plan

The Fall 2026 course plan accounts for Monday holidays, the October 5 student holiday, reading week and the in-class exam.

| Week | Date | Planned work | Pedagogical marker |
|----|----|----|----|
| 1 | August 31 | Module 1 - Setup, RStudio, Quarto and first reproducible report. | Install the technical foundations and produce a first trace. |
| 2 | September 7 | Holiday, Labour Day. Required preparatory work for Module 2. | Prepare GitHub, RStudio, Excel import, `dplyr`, `ggplot2` and the mini-test. |
| 3 | September 14 | Module 2 - GitHub, Excel import, transformation with `dplyr` and first charts. | Use in class what was prepared during the previous week. |
| 4 | September 21 | Module 3 - Categories, strings, counts and visualization. | Move from raw data to a clear descriptive question. |
| 5 | September 28 | Module 4 - Factors, cleaning, recoding and imperfect data. | Targeted office hours on Modules 1 to 4. |
| 6 | October 5 | Student holiday, provincial election. | Office hours or help clinic to consolidate Modules 1 to 4 before the exam. |
| 7 | October 12 | Holiday, Thanksgiving. | Independent exam preparation. |
| 8 | October 19 | In-class exam. | Individual autonomy on the essential skills from Modules 1 to 4. |
| 9 | October 26 | Reading week. | Rest, catch-up and consolidation before the rest of the session. |
| 10 | November 2 | Module 5 - Relationships between variables. | Compare, visualize and interpret associations carefully. |
